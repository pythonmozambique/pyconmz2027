PyCon Mozambique 2027 is the first Python conference in the country. It is organised by the Python Mozambique community.

Dates and the venue are still to be announced.

The conference is a place for maintainers, students, and working developers to share talks, run workshops, and spend time on open-source sprints.

Talks can be given in English or Portuguese.
