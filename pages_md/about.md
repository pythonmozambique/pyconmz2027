PyCon Mozambique 2027 is the first Python conference in the country. It is organised by the Python Mozambique community and runs from 14 to 17 October 2027 in Maputo.

The conference is a place for maintainers, students, and working developers to share talks, run workshops, and spend a day on open-source sprints.

Talks can be given in English or Portuguese.
