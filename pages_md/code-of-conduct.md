PyCon Mozambique 2027 exists so people can learn and collaborate. Harassment, discrimination, and deliberate intimidation are not part of that.

This applies to talks, sprints, social events, and the conference's online spaces.

If something is wrong, contact the organisers at python.community.moz@gmail.com. Reports are handled by the organising team and are not published.

We expect participants to follow the spirit of the [Python Software Foundation code of conduct](https://policies.python.org/python.org/code-of-conduct/).
