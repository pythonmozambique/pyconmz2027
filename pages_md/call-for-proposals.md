The call for proposals closes on 15 May 2027.

You can submit a 30-minute talk, a 90-minute tutorial, or a lightning talk. Keynotes are invited by the programme team.

1. Create an account on this site.
2. Open your profile and choose **Submit talk proposal**.
3. Write the abstract in Markdown. Notes on the form stay private.

Submissions are reviewed in the wafer talk tools. Accepted talks are what the public schedule and the home page read from the database.
