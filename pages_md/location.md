PyCon Mozambique 2027 is in Maputo.

- **14 October** — workshops and sprints at the Centro Cultural Franco-Moçambicano (CCFM).
- **15–16 October** — the main conference at the Centro de Conferências Joaquim Chissano, Avenida Marginal.
- **17 October** — open-source sprints.

Travel and room details will be updated on this page as the local team confirms them.
